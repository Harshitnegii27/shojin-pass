## How to review

Every entry needs **two different reviewers** before release (spec 8.5). Until then the server
treats it as unreviewed, and in production every label comes out amber. For each entry check:

1. **Meaning:** is the English meaning right for how the word is used on Japanese labels?
2. **Spellings:** is every spelling really this ingredient? Anything missing (kanji, kana, katakana)?
3. **Tags and diets:** are the "Red" and "Amber" diets right? Red means definitely avoided; amber means
   "ask staff" (source not stated, or some people avoid it). When unsure, amber is the safe choice;
   green is never a guess.
4. Tick the box and write your name. When an entry has two names, a teammate adds both names to its
   `reviewedBy` list in `rulebook/ingredients.json` in a pull request, runs
   `node scripts/validateRulebook.js`, bumps `meta.json` and adds a line to `rulebook/CHANGELOG.md`.
   Corrections (wrong tag, missing spelling) go in the same kind of pull request.

Composite entries (marked *composite*) are words for mixtures, such as だし or キムチ, whose real
contents are not stated on the label.

- `avoid_fungus`: Avoid mushrooms, yeast and koji (red: fungus)
- `avoid_alcohol`: Avoid alcohol (red: alcohol)
